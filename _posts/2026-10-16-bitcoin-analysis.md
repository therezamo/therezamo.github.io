Most traders get chopped up when timeframes fight. That's exactly what's happening with Bitcoin right now at $82,413. We are looking at a genuine multi-timeframe conflict.

If you zoom out, the daily structure is clearly bullish. We had a Change of Character (CHoCH) at $66,956, and the weekly rating screams "Buy." But if you zoom in to the 4H chart—the timeframe where most execution actually happens—the sellers are in control. We saw a bearish CHoCH at $82,563, followed by a bearish Break of Structure (BOS) at $81,033. The ADX is sitting at 36.5, indicating a strong trend. 

Price is trading in the premium zone on both timeframes, about 5.6% below the structural high at $87,395. So we have two bullish timeframes, one bearish timeframe, and zero neutral. The catch? That single bearish timeframe is the dominant one for short-term execution.

Here is how I am reading the next 2 to 3 weeks. Neither of these are immediate entry signals—neither setup offers a clean 2:1 risk-to-reward ratio right now. These are roadmaps.

### The Bearish Scenario (Currently Dominant)
- **Trigger:** A 4H close below $81,033 (the swing low and daily Fib 0.5).
- **The Path:** If that level breaks, we look at $79,715 (daily Fib 0.618), then into the unfilled 4H Fair Value Gap (FVG) between $78,490 and $80,555. The ultimate target is $75,600.
- **Invalidation:** A 4H close above $84,881.
- **The Risk:** There are two unmitigated bearish order blocks sitting directly overhead.

### The Bullish Scenario
- **Trigger:** A 4H close above $82,875 with above-average volume.
- **The Path:** $83,146, up into the unmitigated bearish order block at $84,168, then $84,463, $84,881, targeting $85,347.
- **Invalidation:** A 4H close below $81,033.
- **The Risk:** Breaking the order block without real volume. That is a classic trap in this kind of structure.

The honest read is that the setup is mixed with a heavy bearish tilt. Confidence is low right now. Everything comes down to two specific levels: $81,033 and $84,881. Everything happening between those two numbers is just noise.
