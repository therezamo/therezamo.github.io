## The honest read

This is genuinely mixed, with a bearish tilt. Confidence: low. Neither structural tool returned a setup above the 2:1 R:R floor at the current price, so these are roadmaps, not entry signals.

The decision comes down to two levels: $81,033 and $84,881. Everything between them is noise.
