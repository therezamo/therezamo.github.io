## Terms used

- **4H:** four-hour candles.
- **FVG (fair value gap):** a price gap left by a fast move, which price often returns to fill.
- **CHoCH (change of character):** the first break of a recent swing point against the prevailing trend, read as an early sign of a possible shift.
- **ADX:** a measure of trend strength, not direction. Readings under about 20 are usually read as a weak or absent trend.
- **SMC (smart money concepts):** a style of market-structure analysis built on order blocks, FVGs and liquidity.
- **R:R:** the ratio of potential reward to risk on a trade.
